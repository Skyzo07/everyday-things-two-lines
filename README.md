Entry for [Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479) — a two-line explanation of one everyday thing for a 10-year-old.

Line 1 explains a traffic jam in a single vivid sentence built on one concrete, surprising analogy. Line 2 says in one sentence what that analogy maps and what it leaves out.

`main.ts` writes the two lines exactly to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), and fails loudly if the text is not 1–400 characters or not exactly two lines.
